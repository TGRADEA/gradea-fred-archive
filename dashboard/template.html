Luck or skill

Search enough strategies and the best one looks good by construction. The dashed line is the Sharpe ratio the best of this search would be expected to reach if none of these strategies worked at all; bars are 90% bootstrap intervals that keep the autocorrelation of daily returns intact. A strategy whose interval straddles that line has not said anything yet.

What the archive holds
